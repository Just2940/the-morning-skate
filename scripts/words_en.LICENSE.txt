scripts/words_en.txt.gz - English word forms used to catch garbled words in
Morning Brief headlines. Generated from the SCOWL-based hunspell en_US
dictionary (stems expanded with their affix rules, lower-cased).

SCOWL (Spell Checker Oriented Word Lists), http://wordlist.aspell.net/
